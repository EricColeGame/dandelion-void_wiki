The store page tells you which one you are looking at. Check the platform label before you buy, and check again before you buy a second copy.

## Performance Modes, Audio, and Controller Settings Worth Changing

Modern console games usually ship with a small set of graphics presets. The names vary, but the trade-offs do not.

| Mode | Typical trade-off | Best suited for |
|---|---|---|
| Performance | Higher frame rate, lower resolution | Responsive movement, action sequences |
| Fidelity | Higher resolution, lower frame rate | Screenshot-worthy environments |
| Balanced | Middle ground on both | Most players, most of the time |
| Ray tracing (if offered) | Heavier lighting load | Slower, exploration-focused play |

If your television supports high refresh rates and variable refresh rate, enable both in the PS5 system settings and turn on your TV's game mode. Community reports consistently show that mismatched TV settings — not the console — cause most perceived input lag. If movement feels "swimmy," check the display before blaming the game.

### Audio and controller settings to try

- **3D audio:** If you play on headphones, test both the on and off states. Some mixes benefit enormously; others sound thin.
- **Haptics intensity:** Reduce it if fine vibration becomes distracting during long, quiet stretches.
- **Trigger resistance:** Lower it for comfort during extended sessions, especially if you have hand fatigue.
- **Controller speaker:** Often best muted for immersive, ambient titles.

Take five minutes in the options menu before your first real session. Adjusting mid-playthrough breaks immersion far more than adjusting up front.

**Is Dandelion Void PS5 available to buy right now?**

**Should I wait for more Dandelion Void PS5 details before buying?**

**Is it better to play on PS5 or PC?**
